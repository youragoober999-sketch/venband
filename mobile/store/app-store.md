## App Review notes
- Provide a demo account (email + password) in App Store Connect.
- User-generated content: users can report messages and people, block users, and moderators/staff act on reports (Guideline 1.2).
- Account deletion is in Settings → Security → Delete account (Guideline 5.1.1(v)).
- Microphone and camera are only used for calls and pictures the user chooses to send.

## Privacy “nutrition label”
- Contact info (email) — linked to the user, used for app functionality.
- User content — collected but end-to-end encrypted; not readable by the developer.
- Identifiers (user ID) — app functionality. No tracking.
