# Google Play listing

**App name:** Venband
**Short description (80):** Private group chat, voice and video — every message end-to-end encrypted.

**Full description:**
Venband is a place to hang out with your friends and communities — servers with text and voice channels, DMs, group chats of up to 15 people, and voice and video calls with screen sharing.

Everything you send is end-to-end encrypted on your phone before it leaves it. Venband’s servers only ever store scrambled text, so nobody else — not even Venband — can read your messages.

• Servers with channels, roles, threads, forums, polls and custom emoji
• Voice and video calls, with a phone-style call screen
• DMs and group chats with reactions, replies, pins and saved messages
• Two-factor sign-in, link-safety warnings and Voogle protection against alt accounts
• Themes, profile frames, animated names and a community Marketplace
• Free and open source

**Category:** Communication · **Content rating:** Teen (user-generated content, moderated)
**Website:** https://www.venband.com · **Privacy policy:** https://www.venband.com/privacy

## Data safety answers
- Data collected: email address, user ID, username/profile (account management); app interactions are not collected for ads.
- Messages, photos, videos, audio and files: **encrypted end-to-end; Venband can’t read them.**
- Data is encrypted in transit: yes. Users can request deletion: yes (Settings → Security → Delete account).
- No data shared with third parties for advertising. No ads.
