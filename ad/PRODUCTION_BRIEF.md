# Venband Ad — Production Brief

## Format
- Duration: 20–25 seconds
- Aspect: 16:9 (1920×1080) for YouTube + 9:16 (1080×1920) for TikTok/Reels
- Style: Clean, modern, premium — white background, black text, subtle gradients. Think Apple product ad meets Stripe marketing. Not flashy; confident and minimal.

## Color palette
- Background: pure white #FFFFFF
- Text: near-black #0A0A0A
- Accent gradient: #5865F2 → #A855F7 → #EC4899 (used sparingly on the logo and CTAs only)
- Secondary text: #555555
- Device chrome: #0B0F19 (dark) so devices pop against white

## Typography
- Font: Inter (or SF Pro / Helvetica Neue)
- Headlines: 900 weight, tight tracking (-0.03em), 72–96px
- Body/sub: 500 weight, 28–32px, #555
- All text readable, never overlapping devices

## Layout rule (critical)
- Text on the LEFT third (max 720px wide), device mockup on the RIGHT third.
- Never let a device overlap text. Use generous whitespace (120px margins).
- Title + subtitle stacked, then optional small caption below.

## Scene timeline (22s total)

### 0:00–0:02 — Intro
- White fade-in. Venband logo (gradient text) scales in with a soft spring. Tagline "Talk with your people. Privately." fades up below.
- Sound: soft chime (C major, two notes).

### 0:02–0:04 — Privacy (the hero moment)
- A clean white modal card slides up from center: rounded 28px, subtle shadow.
- Shield icon (gradient circle) at top.
- Headline: "We Protect Your Privacy Because It's Yours"
- Body: "End-to-end encrypted. Your messages, files, and calls are yours alone — even we can't read them."
- Sound: gentle whoosh + soft pop.

### 0:04–0:07 — Message speed
- Two phone mockups side by side. A message bubble animates from the left phone to the right phone (slide + fade). Label: "Instant messages. Device to device in milliseconds." Sub: "Real-time, no lag."
- Sound: quick tick-tick-tick (3 short beeps).

### 0:07–0:10 — Servers & channels
- Desktop mockup slides in from the right. Sidebar (channels) + main chat area with messages. Label: "Servers, channels, roles." Sub: "Like Discord — but open source and yours."
- Sound: soft chime.

### 0:10–0:13 — Voice/video/screen share
- Tablet mockup with a 2×2 grid of video tiles. Label: "Voice, video & screen share." Sub: "Peer-to-peer. No middleman."
- Sound: pop + soft tone.

### 0:13–0:15 — Files
- Text only (no device). "Files up to 5 GB." Sub: "Stored on your site. Yours forever."
- Sound: whoosh.

### 0:15–0:17 — Customization
- Text only. "Themes, GIFs, bots." Sub: "Make it yours."
- Sound: chime.

### 0:17–0:22 — CTA
- Logo + "Venband" large. Tagline "Free & open source. Download now." URL www.venband.com.
- Sound: full chime (4 notes, ascending).

## Device mockups
- Phone: 260×520, rounded 38px, dark screen (#0B0F19) with status bar dots and message bubbles. Must look like a real phone.
- Desktop: 760×460, rounded 16px, sidebar + chat. Must look like a real app window.
- Tablet: 380×500, rounded 26px, 2×2 video grid.

## Animation style
- Easing: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) (smooth, premium)
- Scene transitions: crossfade 0.6s
- Elements: slide in from left/right/up with 0.9s duration, staggered 0.1–0.15s
- Devices: subtle float (3.5s, ±18px, infinite)
- No jarring cuts. Everything eases.

## Sound design
- Soft, premium, Apple-like. Sine waves only, no harsh frequencies.
- Volumes: 0.08–0.12. Never loud.
- Each scene has a distinct, short sound (0.2–0.5s).
- Final CTA: ascending 4-note chime (C-E-G-C).

## What NOT to do
- No dark background (white only).
- No text overlapping devices.
- No Discord branding or copy.
- No cluttered layouts. Whitespace is a feature.
- No auto-playing loud sounds.

## Deliverables
- MP4 (H.264 + AAC), 1920×1080, 22s
- MP4 (H.264 + AAC), 1080×1920, 22s (vertical)
- WebM (VP9 + Opus) for web embedding
- Thumbnail: 1280×720 with logo + "Talk privately. Free & open source."

## Reference
- Apple product ads (clean, minimal, confident)
- Stripe marketing (whitespace, subtle motion)
- Linear (premium, precise)
- Discord (structure only, not visuals)
