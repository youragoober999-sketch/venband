Plugin ecosystem

A growing library of plugins — from message logging and better notifications to productivity tools and UI tweaks. Toggle the ones you love, ignore the rest.

02

Theme it your way

Accent colors, custom CSS, and full theme packs. Venband injects your styles right at startup, so the app looks like yours the second it opens.

03

Private by default

No telemetry, no data off your machine. Venband is fully local and open source — inspect every line if you want to. Your Disband stays your Disband.

04

Up-to-date, always

Automatic updates track the latest Disband releases, so your mods keep working after every app update. One click and you’re current.

05

A single script installs Venband in seconds — no account, no build step, no acrobatics. From zero to modded in under a minute.
